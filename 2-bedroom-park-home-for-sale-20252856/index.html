THE HOME

Situated on Broxburn Park in South Hykeham, this brand-new 36' x 20' Omar Westbury offers approximately 678 sq. ft. of bright and thoughtfully arranged single-storey accommodation. Finished in an attractive neutral colour scheme, the home combines a generous L-shaped lounge and dining room with a spacious contemporary kitchen, two bedrooms, a separate bathroom and an excellent dressing room leading to an en-suite shower room.

Externally, the home has a smart cream finish beneath a pitched tiled roof, complemented by white-framed double-glazed windows and brick-effect skirting. Steps rise to the principal entrance, while a second external door provides convenient direct access to the kitchen.

The impressive L-shaped lounge and dining room extends across one end of the home and creates a wonderfully sociable space for relaxing, dining and entertaining. The dining area measures approximately 19'10" x 9'2", providing ample room for a full-size dining table and accompanying furniture. Several windows allow plenty of natural light to enter, while the vaulted ceiling enhances the excellent feeling of height and space.

The adjoining lounge area measures approximately 9'2" x 8'11" and is comfortably proportioned for a selection of seating and occasional furniture. Its open connection with the dining area provides a versatile arrangement without sacrificing the sense of clearly defined living zones. The neutral decoration, coordinating carpet and extensive glazing create a bright and welcoming interior that is ready for its first owner to enjoy.

The separate kitchen measures approximately 12'8" x 11'4" at its maximum and is fitted with a comprehensive range of contemporary wall and base units. These are complemented by wood-effect work surfaces and attractive marble-effect splashbacks. The well-planned arrangement provides generous cupboard and preparation space, together with a sink and drainer positioned beneath the window. Fitted appliances include an oven, hob and extractor, fridge, freezer, washing machine and a dishwasher.

Two windows bring excellent natural light into the kitchen, while the wood-effect flooring offers a smart and easily maintained finish. A glazed external door provides useful everyday access to the outside.

An inner hallway connects the living accommodation with both bedrooms and the bathroom. One bedroom measures approximately 9'1" x 8' at its maximum and benefits from an adjoining dressing room. This valuable separate space provides excellent storage without requiring large wardrobes to unnecessarily encroach upon the bedroom itself. Beyond the dressing room is a private en-suite shower room, fitted with a curved glazed shower enclosure, pedestal wash basin and WC. A window provides natural light and ventilation, while coordinated wall finishes and wood-effect flooring create a clean, modern appearance.

The second bedroom measures approximately 9'2" x 8'3" and is also capable of accommodating a double bed. It would make an inviting guest bedroom while retaining the flexibility to become a hobby room or home office if preferred. The separate guest bathroom is fitted with a panelled bath, pedestal wash basin and WC. Contemporary wall panelling, a window and practical wood-effect flooring complete the room.

Outside the home benefits from a small garden area to the side and the rear of the home which is mainly laid to lawn with a block paved driveway located to one side of the home. Fences can be erected(subject to the park owners approval) to enclose the garden space if required.

With its brand-new presentation, spacious living accommodation, contemporary kitchen, two bathroom facilities and valuable dressing room, this impressive Omar Westbury provides an excellent opportunity to enjoy comfortable and easily managed park-home living.

THE PARK

Broxburn Park is an established residential development situated in the peaceful surroundings of South Hykeham, approximately four miles south-west of Lincoln. Designed exclusively for residents aged 45 and over, the park offers a friendly community atmosphere with landscaped plots, private parking and manageable garden areas. Published park information also confirms that pets are permitted, subject to the park rules, while mains gas and electricity are available.

Local amenities are conveniently accessible. Pennells Garden Centre and restaurant are approximately 0.3 miles away, while the Asda superstore in North Hykeham, which incorporates a pharmacy and opticians, is around 1.6 miles from the park. The nearest medical centre is approximately three miles away.

The nearest bus stops are on Thorpe Lane, approximately 0.1–0.2 miles away, with services including routes to North Hykeham and Lincoln. Hykeham railway station is approximately 1.7 miles away, providing useful connections towards Lincoln and Nottingham.

Broxburn Park is also well positioned for the A46, Lincoln city centre and the lakes, walking trails and wildlife of nearby Whisby Nature Park, making it an appealing choice for peaceful residential living without feeling isolated.

Pets are allowed on the park (maximum of 2 dogs or 2 cats are permitted).
